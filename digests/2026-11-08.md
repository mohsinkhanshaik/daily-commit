# AI Pulse - 2026-11-08

A weekend read on a week where AI capital was repriced downward while compute and power deals kept getting bigger.

## OpenAI closes an $11B first tranche at a $1.25T valuation
OpenAI received the first $11 billion of a new round valued at $1.25 trillion, below the $1.4 trillion it first sought, and the round may now top out near $20 billion versus an earlier target above $30 billion. Why it matters: even the category leader is accepting a lower price and a smaller check, a sign that investors are pricing in execution risk after the recent tech selloff.
Source: The AI Insider (https://theaiinsider.tech/2026/11/07/openai-secures-11b-at-1-25t-valuation-as-funding-round-falls-short-of-earlier-target)

## Anthropic IPO talks slip to $1.2T-$1.3T
Investors reportedly now expect Anthropic to list at roughly $1.2 trillion to $1.3 trillion, below an earlier $1.5 trillion target, and some questioned whether the roadshow can start next week. ProShares already plans a 2x ETF (ANTU) and a -2x ETF (ANTY) once the shares trade. Why it matters: the largest AI IPO yet is becoming the market's live test of how much frontier-lab revenue growth is worth.
Source: Daily AI Brief (https://dailyaibrief.com/newsletters/daily-ai-brief/2026-11-07-openai-raises-11-billion-at-a-1-25-trillion-valuation-anthropic-ipo-faces-delay-risk-and-more-daily-ai-brief-577)

## Thinking Machines ships Inkling 2 Small
Thinking Machines Lab released Inkling 2 Small, a mixture-of-experts model with 594 billion total and 17 billion active parameters per token that accepts image inputs and is available for inference and fine-tuning through Tinker. The weights are planned for release under Apache 2.0 after external safety testing. Why it matters: a large open-weight MoE from a well-funded lab would add fresh pressure on closed-model pricing.
Source: AI Impact Hub (https://www.aiimpacthub.com/ai-news/2026-11-07)

## Google cuts Gemini Omni video pricing and buys a Wyoming site
Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash is generally available with clips up to 15 seconds, up from 10, and 720p generation now costs $0.07 per second, a 30 percent cut from Omni 1.1. Separately Google acquired Crusoe's unfinished data center site in southeast Wyoming, where it plans to invest $17 billion. Why it matters: Google is pairing aggressive price cuts with the capex needed to sustain them.
Source: AI Impact Hub (https://www.aiimpacthub.com/ai-news/2026-11-07)

## Gigawatt campuses and fuel cells reshape AI power
SpaceXAI applied to build a campus needing up to 6GW on 747 acres in Cochise County, Arizona, with a county decision set for December 9, while NTT Global Data Centers will deploy 900 MW of Bloom Energy fuel cells as primary onsite power, with first shipments in 2027. Why it matters: power, not chips, is becoming the binding constraint, and operators are moving generation behind the meter.
Source: Daily AI Brief (https://dailyaibrief.com/newsletters/daily-ai-brief/2026-11-07-openai-raises-11-billion-at-a-1-25-trillion-valuation-anthropic-ipo-faces-delay-risk-and-more-daily-ai-brief-577)

## EU launches a EUR 525M chip design platform
The European Commission launched a EUR 525 million platform, with EUR 440 million in grants for chip design startups and small firms and EUR 85 million for cloud infrastructure, support services and open-source tools. Why it matters: Europe is betting on the design layer, where it can compete, rather than trying to match leading-edge fabs.
Source: Daily AI Brief (https://dailyaibrief.com/newsletters/daily-ai-brief/2026-11-07-openai-raises-11-billion-at-a-1-25-trillion-valuation-anthropic-ipo-faces-delay-risk-and-more-daily-ai-brief-577)

## Democrats prepare AI evaluation and liability bills
Fresh from midterm gains, Democrats are preparing bills that would require independent evaluations of advanced AI models, clarify corporate liability for AI harms and address data center infrastructure demands, while Bernie Sanders renewed calls for national and local data center moratoriums. Why it matters: the next Congress could move US AI policy from voluntary commitments toward mandatory testing.
Source: Daily AI Brief (https://dailyaibrief.com/newsletters/daily-ai-brief/2026-11-07-openai-raises-11-billion-at-a-1-25-trillion-valuation-anthropic-ipo-faces-delay-risk-and-more-daily-ai-brief-577)

Trend watch: valuations are compressing at the top (OpenAI at $1.25T, Anthropic at $1.2T-$1.3T) while physical buildout keeps scaling (6GW, 900 MW, $17B), and policy is increasingly aimed at that buildout, continuing the capital-versus-power tension running through recent digests.
