# AI Pulse - 2026-11-22
The week AI news became market news: a record frontier-lab IPO, a sharp chip selloff, and a reshuffled accelerator supply chain.

## Anthropic closes its IPO at $83 a share
Anthropic closed its IPO on Nov 20, selling about 1.127 billion Class A shares at $83 including the underwriters' full 147 million share option, for roughly $93.5B in gross proceeds on top of an $81.3B base deal. Early trading figures conflicted across outlets, with reports of both a premium to the offer price and a weak open. Why it matters: a frontier lab now reports to public shareholders, which will make the economics of training and serving models far more visible.
Source: Semafor (https://www.semafor.com/article/11/19/2026/anthropic-makes-record-market-debut)

## Nasdaq and Nvidia slide in a week-long AI selloff
The Nasdaq 100 fell 5.6% on Friday Nov 20 for a weekly loss of about 10.4%, and Nvidia dropped roughly 22.9% on the week to $125.53, according to FT reporting. Taiwan's TAIEX closed down 6.23% at 36,033.29 the same day, and Taiwan's regulator restricted short-selling from Nov 24. Why it matters: the AI trade is now large enough that a single week of doubt moves national indexes and prompts regulatory intervention.
Source: Financial Times (https://www.ft.com/content/9f3c6ed6-c08f-4710-9683-ee2f5eb25841)

## Nvidia stops shipping licensed Hopper chips to China
Nvidia said on its Nov 17 earnings call that it sold $2.4B of licensed Hopper data-center products to China in Q3, but its guidance includes none and it is no longer shipping them. The US had approved a Hopper variant for China last year. Why it matters: even licensed sales to China are drying up, so Nvidia's growth now rests almost entirely on US, European and Gulf buildouts.
Source: Bloomberg Government (https://news.bgov.com/bloomberg-government-news/nvidia-stops-selling-ai-chips-to-china-despite-us-approval)

## Google reportedly trims 2027 Broadcom TPU orders by about 20%
Analyst Ming-Chi Kuo reported that Google is cutting about 1 million units, roughly 20% of planned 2027 Broadcom-designed TPU 8i shipments, and shifting volume toward MediaTek-designed TPU 8t parts. Broadcom fell 11.4% and MediaTek about 10% on the report, which Google has not confirmed. Why it matters: hyperscalers are playing custom-silicon partners against each other, and design wins are no longer sticky.
Source: Stock Analysis (https://stockanalysis.com/news/broadcom-falls-google-tpu-cut-mediatek/)

## OpenAI hardens GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol against distillation
OpenAI said new safeguards in GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol cut adaptive red-team success at extracting protected reasoning from 54.0% to 0.2% on earlier requests and from 22.9% to 0.9% on active ones. The post links attempted extraction to activity associated with DeepSeek and Moonshot AI. Why it matters: model outputs are now treated as defended IP, and distillation is becoming a front in US-China AI competition.
Source: OpenAI (https://openai.com/index/combatting-adversarial-distillation/)

## Gemini learns to operate a Mac
Google added a Computer Use mode to the Gemini app on macOS that runs in a background window, switches apps, edits local files and works across web pages, pausing for logins and payments. It needs an Apple Silicon Mac on macOS 15 or later plus a Google AI Plus, Pro or Ultra plan, and launches US-only in English. Why it matters: desktop agents are moving from developer previews into paid consumer subscriptions.
Source: TechCrunch (https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/19/google-gives-gemini-the-ability-to-use-your-mac-for-you/)

## Thinking Machines ships Inkling 2 Mini open weights
Inkling 2 Mini is a 26.4B parameter open-weights model with 3.4B active parameters per token that runs on a 32GB MacBook Pro via vLLM, Ollama or SGLang. The lab reports 77.9% on SWE-bench Pro v2 Hard and 48.2% on DeepSWE v1.1. Why it matters: capable coding models keep shrinking onto laptops, which pressures API pricing at the low end.
Source: Thinking Machines (https://thinkingmachines.ai/news/inkling-2-mini/)

Trend watch: capital and compute are being repriced at once, as a record IPO, a 10% Nasdaq week, vanishing China chip sales and a TPU order shift all point to investors demanding proof of returns from the AI buildout that has driven this archive's funding and compute stories for weeks.
